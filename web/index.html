Data & method

Data. Daily station observations for April 2026 come from the Hong Kong Observatory:

  • daily mean temperature
  • daily total rainfall
  • station locations

The land mask uses the official 18-district boundary. The timeline's Hong Kong rainfall total sums the 21 displayed station totals; mean temperature averages the same stations.

Interpolation. Temperature uses exact k-nearest IDW (p = 1.5, k = 20). Rainfall uses exact local IDW after a log1p transform (p = 2.5, k = 8). Parameters were selected by leave-one-station-out validation.

Only the 30 daily station records are observations. Values between stations and between dates are estimates; the smooth timeline does not represent measured hourly data.
